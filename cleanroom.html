Privacy & Data Flow

Local cleanup. Reversible local archives.

Evidence-backed behavior for Cleanroom Windows v1.0.7.

01

Stored on your device

Scanned temporary files are archived into %APPDATA%\Cleanroom\Archive\ before removal. Archive manifests record original file paths to support exact one-click restoration.

02

Network boundary

No application network path was found in the audited public v1.0.7 source and packaged dependency surface. Performs zero background uploads or cloud requests.

03

Telemetry & retention

Zero background telemetry or cloud analytics. Archived files remain safely on local disk until restored or explicitly purged by the operator.
