What it does

Archive-first cleanup that treats your files as evidence, not garbage.

Archive-first

Before anything is removed, it is archived. You clean up without the risk of an irreversible delete.

Receipts & custody checks

Every action produces a receipt with custody checks, so you can trace what was moved and where it landed.

Rollback

If a cleanup step was wrong, rollback puts things back. Proof of what happened is kept either way.

No cloud, no account, no telemetry

Runs locally. No sign-in. No telemetry. Your file inventory never leaves your machine.
