What it does

Archive-first cleanup that treats your files as evidence, not garbage.

Archive-first containment

Before anything is removed from active paths, it is archived into a sealed container. Cleanup happens without the risk of irreversible loss.

Receipts & custody checks

Every cleanup run generates a `.cleanroom-receipt` with SHA-256 custody checks, recording what was moved and where it landed.

Restore capability

If a cleanup step needs to be reversed, 1-click restore puts files back into their recorded original paths, keeping proof of the action.

No cloud, no account, no telemetry

Runs strictly on your machine. No sign-in required, zero telemetry collected. Your file inventory never leaves your system.
