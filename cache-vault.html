What it does

Clipboard history that stays on your machine, with receipts for everything.

Clipboard history & safes

Capture every clip automatically, then organize the ones that matter into named safes and collections.

Global search & Quick Paste

Find any clip fast across history and safes, and paste it back in one step with Quick Paste.

Command Center

Trigger actions from a single hotkey-driven launcher. Phase 1 ships Hotkey Actions; expansions wait for deliberate scope decisions.

Screenshots & images

Images live alongside text clips, so visual context is never lost between pastes.

Exports & proof packs

Bundle clips into exports and proof-pack workflows with stamped receipts you can inspect later.

Local-first storage

No cloud account, no telemetry. Your clipboard vault lives on your machine in durable files you own.

Mobile companion

An opt-in LAN-only Mobile Bridge pairs Cache Vault Desktop with a production-signed Android companion. No cloud relay — your clips stay on the local network.

Why Cache Vault

Differentiators, not hype.

Receipts, not just history

Every clip and export can carry a stamped receipt — proof of what was captured and when.

User-owned workflows

Durable local files mean your vault is not hostage to a SaaS shutdown or a pricing change.

Honest claims

What Cache Vault does is tied to tested behavior and visible release proof, not marketing language.

Founder license gate

Advanced features are unlocked offline with an Ed25519 Founder license. No online account or subscription is required for the usable base application.
