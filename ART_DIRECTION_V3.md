## Motion

Ordinary hyperlinks change real documents. A selected component engages; its snapshot carries between compatible mounting surfaces while the departing scene withdraws and the next world arrives. The product/object determines the movement. At ordinary junctions, the incoming opaque room covers the withdrawing document plane; the outgoing content stays visible until covered. Native Back/Forward retains static browser history and scroll restoration: its transition is skipped after a reproducible Chrome history-reveal race. Only the exact outgoing ready rejection caused by that skip is handled; other reasons remain errors. Selected-link travel remains animated. Record and inspect intermediate frames and normal-speed playback. Reduced motion, no-JS and unsupported browsers retain direct native navigation.
